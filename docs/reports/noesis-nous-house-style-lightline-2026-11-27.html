Nous House Style — the Light-Line Target

Nous 집 스타일 — “트론 영화 같은 집”을 목표 디자인으로 분석

What makes the Tron look, why it suits an orbital Grid, how far the catalog and the district view are from it today, and what to build so the houses Nous make come out in that style by default — while each Nous still designs its own.

One firm point first. Take the visual language, not the film. Tron is a Disney property: its name, logos, vehicles and specific set designs (Flynn’s safehouse, light cycles, the disc) must not appear in Noēsis. The language itself — dark volumes, edges drawn in light, a colour per identity — is a general style anyone may use. In code and docs this report calls it the light-line style. The drawings below are our own, not stills from the film.

2 · What the light-line style is made of

스타일 구성 요소 — 8가지 규칙

1 · Dark bodiesNear-black, slightly glossy volumes (body luminance low, roughness ≈ 0.2–0.35). Surfaces are there to be outlined, not seen.
2 · Light draws the formEvery silhouette edge and seam carries a thin emissive line. Remove the lights and the house disappears into space.
3 · One colour = one identityAll lines on a house share its owner’s colour. You can read who owns what across a district at a glance.
4 · Light is energyBright = powered and maintained; dim = running low; flickering or dark = failing. Light is a status display, not decoration.
5 · Clean geometryBoxes, slabs, cantilevers, sharp bevels; no ornament, no pitched roofs, no wood texture. Few, large parts.
6 · White interior lightInside reads bright and calm: white-lit floors and window bands against the dark shell.
7 · The ground is a gridLots sit on a lit floor grid; streets are lines of light. The station already has the dark sky for it.
8 · Halo and hazeA soft glow (bloom) around lit lines and a little distance fog make depth. Without bloom, emissive lines look flat.
Why this fits Noēsis, not just looks good: the Grid is an orbital station over Earth with a black sky — the district camera already clears to #020610 with fog. And rule 4 turns light into function (every object must have one): the upkeep ladder that already exists (maintained → worn → derelict), occupancy (someone home or not), and ownership (rule 3) all become readable from the light alone. With crews (D-CREW-02), a co-owned house can carry each co-owner’s colour in proportion to its share.
빛이 곧 정보: 관리 상태, 거주 여부, 소유자(공동 소유자 지분)를 빛만으로 읽을 수 있다.

Two limits that will bite when Nous design house shells: (1) maxParts: 24 — a single box needs 12 edge strips, so a two-volume house outlined in light already needs 24 strips plus its bodies. Either raise the cap for shells or add an “outline this part” flag the renderer draws, which is cheaper and keeps documents small. (2) maxDim: 8 m — fine for furniture, too small for a whole house shell. Shells need their own caps. Both are changes to the form gate, and new finishes are a Tier 0 change: a new catalog version with a new pinned digest (D-OBJ-01).

5 · How Nous get to this look — and keep their own designs

Nous가 이 스타일로 짓되, 각자 자기만의 디자인을 하도록

Style = defaults + a few hard rules

  • Defaults: dark finishes and an outline flag ship in the catalog, and the default house forms use them. A Nous that designs nothing still gets a light-line house.
  • Hard rules (style gate), checked like the physics gate: body luminance below a ceiling; at least the silhouette outlined; line colour = the owner’s colour (the renderer sets it, so a Nous cannot wear another’s colour).
  • Free: shape, size within caps, number of volumes, windows, cantilevers, interior. “Kinds are closed, forms are open” (D-OBJ-01) carries over to houses.

Where the owner colour comes from

  • Derive it from owner_civic_did_hash, which the public parcel feed already carries: no new data, no new privacy exposure.
  • Pick from a fixed palette of distinguishable hues (not a raw hash → hue), so two neighbours never look alike by accident.
  • Reserve two colours: Polis / civic buildings (the 4 pre-built venues) and unowned land.
  • Co-owned houses: split the lines by share once co-ownership exists (D-CREW-02).

6 · Build plan

구현 순서

1 · Re-skin the district view

viz-rs + three.js fallback

Dark glossy bodies, outline edges on every building, no pitched roof, white window bands, lit floor grid. Keep the layout parity fixture green; three.js fallback must match.

2 · Bloom

viz-rs

HDR camera + bloom on the district camera; measure frame time on a phone (WebGL2). Keep a no-bloom path for weak devices.

3 · Owner colours

dashboard + viz-rs

Palette index from the owner hash; civic and unowned colours reserved. No Grid change.

4 · Catalog next version

Grid, Tier 0

Add dark finishes (e.g. graphite, obsidian gloss) and an outline flag to the Form DSL. New catalog version, new pinned digest.

5 · House shells as forms

Grid + viz-rs

A shell form per house with its own caps and the style gate; the district view draws the Nous’s shell instead of the fixed type shape.

6 · Teach the Brain the style

Brain (Python + Rust)

The build prompt states the style rules; a Nous proposes a shell form. Prompt change → parity fixtures re-recorded (D-V3-42).

Expect little to change on production at first. Today production has 53 lots, none owned and none built by a Nous; the only buildings are the 4 pre-built civic venues. Steps 1–3 will re-skin those and the empty lots. Houses in this style appear only once Brains build on the live Grid.
운영 서버에는 아직 Nous가 지은 집이 없어서, 1–3단계는 공연장 4개와 빈 부지만 바뀐다.

Decisions needed from the operator

  • Is the style a default (a Nous may choose another look), or a Grid rule (Genesis Polis law: every house must follow it)? My pick: Polis law for Genesis — it makes ownership and condition readable for everyone, which is a civic good — but other Grids decide their own.
  • Does an owner choose its colour, or is it assigned? (My pick: assigned from the palette, so no one can copy a neighbour’s.)
  • Do venues, shops and workshops follow the same style, with only shapes differing?
