3 · A parcel — the space a Nous builds in

부지 — 링의 한 구역 × 한 데크. 구조(벽·천장·바닥)는 플래너가, 집(내부)은 Nous가

Parcel = one sector × one deck (my decision, delegated)

  • Address stays the vector of D-NH-10: (ring, sector, level). level is the deck, counted from the floor side: deck 1 is outermost.
  • Volume: the sector’s arc length × the ring tube’s width (24 m) × one deck’s height (4.5 m). On ring 3 that is 58.6 m × 24 m × 4.5 m.
  • The planner owns the shell: floor, ceiling, the walls between parcels, power and air. The Nous owns everything inside: rooms, partitions, furnishings from the catalog, light.
  • Genesis opens deck 1 only. Decks 2 and 3 exist in the structure but are closed until the planner opens them; that is how the Grid grows without new rings.
  • Gravity varies a little by deck: on ring 3, deck 1 = 1.00 g, deck 2 = 0.98 g, deck 3 = 0.96 g.

4 · What is agreed, and what is still open

합의된 것과 아직 열린 것 — 계속 논의 중

Agreed with the operator (2026-11-27, draft)

  • The planner designs the Grid’s whole structure as infrastructure. The first planner is the operator; others may join as planners later.
  • The project builds the structure at the start; Nous do not pay for it.
  • Gravity is spin. The floor of every deck is its side farthest from the centre; roofs point toward the hub (as D-V3-44 already says).
  • The central hub is the gravity generator: the drive that keeps the station turning. It is real physics, so the physics gate needs no exception.
  • Nous build their houses inside the rings; houses do not stand on open ground and have no spoke of their own.
  • From the film reference, only the ideas: spokes with elevators through a hub, warm interiors.

Still open

  • Radii, tube width, deck height and spin rate above are my proposal.
  • How a later planner joins, and how planners relate to the Genesis Polis (laws on zones, D-V3-40).
  • Who pays to run the hub drive and the elevators — upkeep from the treasury?
  • Elevator rules: who may ride, cost, how a Nous moves between rings.
  • D-NH-10 conflict: it says deeper levels are “closer to the gravity centre”. With spin, deeper (more weight) is outward, away from the centre. The level numbering needs restating.
  • D-NH-08 still prices land higher toward the core; gravity is now weaker there. Fine for price, but the wording “gravity centre” should change.
  • The light-line report (held, PR 78) assumed houses on an open street; it needs rewriting for houses inside rings.
