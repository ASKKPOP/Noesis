Genesis Grid — Structure v0 draft

첫 Grid 시각화 — 중심 척추(허브·회전 구동부), 스포크에 매달린 3개의 긴 거주 팔, 팔 안의 부지에 Nous가 집을 짓는다

The planner designs the whole structure and the project builds it: a central spine carrying the hub and spin drive, the power plant at one end and the Government Core at the other, and three long habitat arms held on spokes. Nous build their houses inside the arms. Gravity comes from spin, so a floor is the side of an arm farthest from the spine. Counts are the live Genesis Grid’s: 1 core, 4 commons, 24 work, 24 home parcels.

1 · The parts

구성 — 척추(동력·허브·운영 링·정부 코어)와 세 개의 거주 팔

Spin gravity a = ω²·r. All three arms hang at the same distance from the spine, so they share one gravity, set to 1 g at 2 rpm. Lengths, radius, deck height and spin rate are my proposal for v0, not settled.

3 · A parcel — the space a Nous builds in

부지 — 팔의 한 구역 × 한 데크. 외피(벽·천장·바닥)는 플래너가, 집(내부)은 Nous가

Parcel = one arm section × one deck (my decision, delegated)

  • Address keeps the shape of D-NH-10: (arm, section, level). The field today called ring would name the arm. level is the deck, counted from the floor side: deck 1 is outermost.
  • Volume: the section’s length along the arm × the arm’s width (40 m) × one deck (4.5 m). A home or work parcel is 17.9 m × 40 m × 4.5 m; a commons hall is 107.5 m long.
  • The planner owns the shell: floor, ceiling, walls between parcels, power and air. The Nous owns everything inside: rooms, partitions, catalog furnishings, light.
  • Genesis opens deck 1 only. Decks 2 and 3 exist but stay closed until the planner opens them; that is how the Grid grows without new arms.
  • Gravity varies a little by deck: deck 1 = 1.00 g, deck 2 = 0.98 g, deck 3 = 0.96 g.

4 · What is agreed, and what is still open

합의된 것과 아직 열린 것 — 계속 논의 중

Agreed with the operator (2026-11-27, draft)

  • The planner designs the Grid’s whole structure as infrastructure. The first planner is the operator; others may join as planners later.
  • The project builds the structure at the start; Nous do not pay for it.
  • Layout: a central spine with three long habitat arms on spokes (the planner’s concept sketch). Our own design and names; no film ship is copied.
  • Gravity is spin. The hub is the gravity generator: the drive that keeps the arms turning. A floor is the side farthest from the spine. Real physics, so the physics gate needs no exception.
  • Nous build their houses inside the arms; houses have no spoke of their own. Nous travel by elevator through the spokes and the hub.
  • From the film reference, only the ideas: spokes with elevators through a hub, warm interiors.

Still open

  • Arm length, radius, width, deck height and spin rate are my proposal.
  • Rings become arms: the live Grid’s parcels use ring 0–3. With arms at one distance, ring no longer means distance from the centre.
  • D-NH-08 prices land by ring (closer to the core = dearer). With all arms at the same distance, that basis is gone; land value needs a new rule (nearness to a spoke or elevator, deck, arm).
  • D-NH-10 says deeper levels are “closer to the gravity centre”. With spin, deeper (heavier) is outward. The level wording needs restating.
  • Who runs the power plant, hub drive and elevators, and from what budget.
  • How a later planner joins; how planners relate to the Genesis Polis (zone law, D-V3-40).
  • The held light-line report (PR 78) assumed houses on an open street; it needs rewriting for houses inside arms.
