Part 2 · What we implemented

Our Nous isn’t built like that assistant. It is one Brain decomposed into cognitive faculties — most of the eight blocks already had a home (persona → psyche, memory → memory, onboarding-wiki → episteme). Three had no faculty. We built those three, in-world first.

aisthesis — perception shipped

αἴσθησις
in-world take on #7 Vision (Supervision) · input edge

Diffs the world-sight feed (parcels + built objects) into salient-change percepts — appeared / vanished / changed — and records what it notices to memory, stirring curiosity. brain/src/noesis_brain/aisthesis/ · 18 tests.

praxis — action shipped

πρᾶξις
in-world take on #5 Interface (sim-use) · output edge

Knows the outward-verb repertoire, validates a chosen action’s shape, and journals every deed the Nous takes — pure observation that never alters the actions. brain/src/noesis_brain/praxis/ · 16 tests.

synopsis — synthesis shipped

σύνοψις
in-world take on #4 Research (Open Notebook) · background

Deterministically consolidates recent memories on the current-goal topic into a persisted digest — split, dedupe, rank by recurrence; no language model. brain/src/noesis_brain/synopsis/ · 12 tests.

Held by decision. The three blocks point at the operator’s real machine (real apps, real camera, real PDFs). Those operator-bridge providers — plus the Type-A-only “control room” to host them — are the held phases 75–76. Nothing on the real machine was touched.
