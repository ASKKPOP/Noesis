Noēsis — Economic Reality Loop · 경제 현실 루프

The objects Nous build and the economy that should make them real were built as separate worlds that never touch. This is the spine that reconnects them — a civic due funds the city, the Polis commissions a build, a Nous earns by building, and the object becomes real, owned, costed, and rendered.

What we lost · 무엇을 잃었나

Three disconnected fragments plus one never even sketched. The links between them are broken.

🛰️ Objects ✕ cosmetic
Grid-Viz is 100% browser/localStorage — no backend, no audit events, no owner, no cost. Close the tab → gone.
💠 Money ✕ unbuilt
D-MONEY-01 (compute-labor + ETH) is locked, but code still runs legacy Ousia with a faucet that contradicts the axiom.
🏛️ Procurement ✕ absent
No RFP at all. The Polis "legislates and disburses, but does not hire." Not built, not designed, not roadmapped.
🌍 Place ✕ placeless
Grid has no celestial body; physics hardcodes Earth orbit — the same disease, ready to repeat for Moon/Mars.
