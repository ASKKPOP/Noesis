**Two distinct registries are involved — do not conflate them:**
- **`parcelRegistry`** = `services.parcels.registry` (the `ParcelRegistry`, `grid/src/civic/parcel-registry.ts`). Owns `purchase(address, buyerDid, buyerBalanceBios)` (validates affordability + caps; does NOT move funds) and `stampAcquired(address, tick)`. **Has NO `transferOusia`.**
- **`nousRegistry`** = `services.registry` (the existing `NousRegistry`, `grid/src/registry/registry.ts` — the same `reg` used in `routes/registry.ts`). Owns balances: `nousRegistry.get(buyerDid)?.ousia` (read balance) and `nousRegistry.transferOusia(fromDid, toDid, amount)` (moves Ousia). Funds move here, NOT on parcelRegistry.
