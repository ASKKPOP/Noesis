**D-NH-06 invariant:** co-work/co-build is ALWAYS paid. Every completed Cowork Agreement settles in Ousia (when funded) or records a mutual-credit IOU (when not) — a completion that pays NOTHING must throw. The IOU ledger is the new bookkeeping substrate (grounded by WIR/Sardex/LETS): v1 is bilateral payables with no interest, no transferability, and a per-pair + global cap. Recording an IOU emits NOTHING on chain; only settlement that MOVES Ousia rides the existing `registry.transferOusia` path. The marketplace v3 (Phase 44) settlement/escrow patterns are the precedent. No new `clock.onTick` — settlement is request-driven.
</context_note>
