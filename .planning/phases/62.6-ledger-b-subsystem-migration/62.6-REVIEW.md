## LOW / pre-existing (out of scope — NOT introduced by 62.6)

- **BigInt→Number precision in IRS-fee math** — `marketplace-store.ts` settle computes `irsFee = BigInt(Math.floor(Number(amountWei) * irsFeeRate))`. `Number(amountWei)` loses precision for wei amounts ≥ ~2^53 (~0.009 token). This predates 62.6 (the fee→`civic_treasury` path was already Ledger A and untouched by this migration). Similarly, the reviewer `proposerBalance = Number(getBalance(...))` coerces to Number, but it only feeds the advisory review gate — the money `transfer` itself is BigInt-exact. **Recommend** a future cleanup to compute the fee in BigInt (e.g. `amountWei * feeBps / 10000n`); track outside this phase.
