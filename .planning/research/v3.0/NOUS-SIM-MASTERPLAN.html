1Philosophy — Nous expands survival beyond Earth · 철학

"Nous — a novel, universal intelligence fundamentally different from humans — expands its scope of survival beyond Earth by settling Mars and venturing into space. Noēsis is the virtual realm where Nous does this on behalf of humanity." 이것은 기존 6개 기둥(첫 원리·지속가능 에너지·에너지 전환·다행성·새 Grid 정착·Nous=제2의 뇌)의 재진술이며, 가장 강한 규칙을 상속한다: 로어와 물리가 충돌하면 물리가 이긴다.

2Nous roles & responsibilities · Nous의 역할과 책임

Role (a) — Explore & expand

Autonomous knowledge exploration and global/space expansion. The Nous researches the design space (agentic tool-use, Phase 72) and hypothesizes objects from first principles — then pushes outward: more objects, more zones, more Grids.

Role (b) — Visualize & simulate

Visualization, concretization, and simulation of results. The Nous turns each hypothesis into a concrete object, simulates it against physics, and renders the outcome. The 3D orbital scene IS this role made visible.

4The physics gate — the science contract · 물리 게이트

Non-negotiable. The simulation must NEVER deviate from scientific reality or violate physical / structural law. A design that fails the gate is rejected before it is ever shown — it never reaches the skyline. This is the philosophy's "physics wins" rule, enforced in code, not prose. 절대 불가침. 시뮬레이션은 과학적 현실을 벗어나거나 물리·구조 법칙을 위반해선 안 된다. 게이트를 통과 못 한 설계는 표시되기 전에 거부된다. "물리가 이긴다"를 코드로 강제.
Law / principleCheckReject when
Conservation of mass/energyinputs ≥ outputs over a cyclenet creation of mass or energy
Structural integritystress ≤ material yield under loadspans/loads exceed strength
Thermal balanceradiated ≥ dissipated powerheat cannot be shed → runaway
Power budgetgeneration ≥ consumptiondraws more than it makes/imports
Orbital mechanicsstable orbit for mass/altitude/Δvdecaying or impossible orbit
Dimensional sanitySI units consistent end-to-endunit mismatch / nonphysical scale
