The whole plan in one line. Build two open-source layers — a synthetic Portal-space (constellation of Grids, not a map of Earth) you click into a city rendered by IsoCity, where each building is AI-generated once and cached so most objects are unique. Nothing Earth-bound, no year-long engine to write. 전체 계획 한 줄: 오픈소스 2개 레이어 — 합성 Portal-space(지구 지도가 아닌 Grid들의 별자리)를 클릭하면 IsoCity가 그리는 도시로 들어가고, 각 건물은 AI가 한 번 생성해 캐시되어 대부분 오브젝트가 고유함. 지구 의존 없음, 1년짜리 엔진 작성 불필요.

Architecture at a glance · 아키텍처 한눈에

Three layers, mirroring your Portal → Grid → Brain structure 1:1. The visualization is just a window onto each layer.

1Our world map — synthetic Noēsis-space · 우리의 세계 지도

Hard rule: Noēsis is built off the real Earth. No MapLibre, Mapbox, Cesium, or lat/lon — those are Earth-bound. The "world" is your own synthetic space, and the Portal is the view of it. 절대 규칙: Noēsis는 실제 지구 밖에 세워집니다. MapLibre·Mapbox·Cesium·위경도 금지(전부 지구 기반). "세계"는 당신만의 합성 공간이고, Portal이 그 뷰입니다.

The world map is the Portal layer: a pannable, zoomable field where each Grid is a node/island floating in Noēsis-space. Genesis is the only lit node today; the framework holds many more (D-V3-04/05/07). This is the Portal — a meta-view of every Grid in your reality.

2Setting · Infra · Resource · Initialization · 설정 · 인프라 · 자원 · 초기화

Infra — what you actually run
  • Hosting: static files — drop into your existing docs/ or dashboard/. No new server.
  • Generation: a fal.ai API key, called only at build/place time.
  • Cache: a per-Grid atlas.json + PNG sheet. Generate-once, reuse forever.
  • Data in: the Grid's existing zone/parcel JSON — no new backend.
Resource — what feeds it
  • Grid JSON — zones, parcels, Civic-DIDs (already produced by your repo).
  • Sprite atlas — grows as buildings are generated; the city's "object catalog".
  • Style seed — one prompt/style so all AI buildings share a coherent look.
  • Fallback art — Kenney CC0 tiles for instant prototyping before any AI call.

3How to — build flow · 만드는 순서

Each step has a concrete done-check. Roughly a weekend to a clickable prototype.

1
Fork the city renderer. Clone amilich/isometric-city (or the lighter victorqribeiro/isocity) into dashboard/grid-viz/. ✓ done: it runs and draws a default city.
2
Define the 6 zones. Replace its zoning palette with Business · Manufacture · Shopping · Residential · Infrastructure · Government Quarter. ✓ done: painting a tile shows the right zone color.
3
Wire Grid JSON → tiles. Write a tiny adapter that reads your Grid's zone/parcel JSON and sets tile state. ✓ done: Genesis Grid data draws the real zone layout.
4
Add AI building generation. Bolt on isometric-city-fal (or your own fal.ai call). On place, generate a unique isometric sprite from the style seed. ✓ done: two same-type buildings look different.
5
Cache to an atlas. Store each generated sprite in atlas.json + PNG; reuse from cache on reload. ✓ done: reload makes zero new API calls.
6
Build the Portal layer. A PixiJS/canvas field with Genesis as a node; click → open the city view. ✓ done: click Genesis → city; back button → Portal.
7
Ship it into docs/. Drop the built page next to your other dashboards, resync any copies. ✓ done: opens in Chrome, no console errors.

4Nous learn & teaching loop · Nous 학습과 가르침

This is the doctrine made literal: the Nous builds the city, the AI generates each building. The "learning" is the Nous improving its building catalog over time; "teaching" is one Nous handing its learned atlas/style to another (or to a newly founded Grid). 교리를 글자 그대로: Nous가 도시를 짓고, AI가 각 건물을 생성한다. "학습"은 Nous가 시간이 지나며 건물 카탈로그를 개선하는 것이고, "가르침"은 한 Nous가 학습한 아틀라스/스타일을 다른 Nous(또는 새로 세워진 Grid)에 넘기는 것입니다.

What "learning" concretely means here

  • Catalog growth — every generated building is kept in the atlas, so the Nous's vocabulary of objects expands as it builds.
  • Style convergence — a fixed style seed + accept/reject keeps new buildings coherent with what came before (evaluate step).
  • Teaching = transfer — exporting that atlas + seed bootstraps a new Grid or a peer Nous, so the next city isn't built from nothing. This is your Nous-builds-Grids doctrine at the visualization layer.

5Example — Genesis Grid walkthrough · 예시

Business 비즈니스 Manufacture 제조 Shopping 쇼핑 Residential 주거 Infrastructure 인프라 Government Quarter 정부구역

Step-by-step, as the user experiences it

  1. Open the dashboard → see Portal-space, Genesis glowing as the one live Grid.
  2. Click Genesis → zoom into the 6-zone isometric city.
  3. The renderer reads Grid JSON, lays out zones, and fills each parcel with a building pulled from the atlas (or generated + cached if new).
  4. Hover a building → tooltip shows its parcel, Civic-DID owner, recent audit events.
  5. As the Grid grows, new unique buildings appear and the atlas — the Nous's learned catalog — grows with it.
